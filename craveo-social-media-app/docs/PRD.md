# Craveo – Project Requirements Document (PRD)

### 1. Problem Statement

Most social media platforms are dominated by generic content such as politics, trending news, or viral memes.  
This makes it difficult for food enthusiasts, amateur cooks, and culinary creators to connect, share, and discover meaningful food-related content.  

**For Users:**  
- Food posts are often buried under unrelated content.  
- Discovering recipes, dishes, or food experiences requires navigating multiple platforms.  
- Engagement with food content is inconsistent due to lack of dedicated communities.

**For Content Creators:**  
- Culinary creators struggle to gain visibility because their content is overshadowed by high-volume general topics.  
- Current platforms do not provide dedicated tools for recipe sharing or food tagging.  

**The Gap:**  
There is a clear need for a **dedicated food-centric social media platform** where content is prioritized, discovery is easy, and engagement is meaningful.



### 2. Project Goals & Objectives

1. **Increase Engagement with Food Content:** Users should interact with posts (like, comment, share) in a way that is intuitive and seamless.  
2. **Improve Discoverability for Culinary Creators:** Recipes, food posts, and culinary stories should be easy to find without interference from unrelated topics.  
3. **Enhance User Experience on Mobile and Web:** Provide a responsive, visually appealing, and intuitive feed to keep users engaged.


### 3. Target User Personas

### Persona 1: Food Enthusiast
- **Name:** Chinedu  
- **Primary Goal:** Discover new dishes, recipes, and food content quickly  
- **Frustration:** Struggles to find quality food content because it is buried among politics, trending news, or general social posts

### Persona 2: Culinary Content Creator
- **Name:** Amaka  
- **Primary Goal:** Share recipes, cooking tips, and food experiences to a targeted audience  
- **Frustration:** Their content is often overshadowed by high-volume general posts; hard to gain visibility and meaningful engagement


### 4. Key Features (Epics)

### Identity & Access Management
- User registration/login (email, social login optional)
- Profile management (bio, avatar, food interests)

### Dynamic Feed & Discovery
- Dedicated food feed displaying posts in real-time
- Filter and search by cuisine, ingredients, and tags
- Infinite scrolling and pagination

### User Interaction
- Like, comment, and share posts
- Save or bookmark recipes/posts
- Notifications for interactions on user content

### Content Creation
- Post creation with images, recipes, or food tips
- Tagging system for ingredients, cuisine, or diet type
- Option to make posts public or private

### Analytics & Engagement
- Simple stats for posts (likes, comments, shares)
- Trending food posts section
- Dashboard for content creators


### 5. MVP Scope

**Goal:** Allow a user to **browse, engage, and post food content in a dedicated feed**.  

**Core Features for MVP:**
- Food-specific feed (dynamic)
- Like, comment, share posts
- Post creation with image and tags
- Profile management
- Infinite scroll for feed

**Parking Lot (Later Enhancements):**
- Advanced analytics for creators
- Recipe saving and meal planning
- Collaborative cooking groups
- Gamification (badges, leaderboards)
- AI-powered recipe suggestions


### 6. Constraints & Considerations
- Must work on **web, mobile, and PWA**
- Backend must support **real-time updates** for likes/comments
- Keep UI **minimal, responsive, and food-focused**
- Avoid generic social noise (politics, memes, trending news)
- Ensure scalability for large amounts of posts and users


### 7. Success Metrics
- User engagement: likes, comments, shares per post
- Creator visibility: ratio of creator content reaching feeds
- Feed responsiveness and page load times
- Growth of active user base within first 30 days
