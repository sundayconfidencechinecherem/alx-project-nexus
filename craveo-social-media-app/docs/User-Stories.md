# Craveo ~ User Stories

This document outlines all **user stories** for the Craveo Food Social Media App.  
Each story follows the standard format:

As a [persona], I want to [action], so that [value]. and stories are grouped by **key features(Epics)**.

## 1. Identity & Access Management

| ID | User Story | Persona |
|----|------------|--------|
| US001 | As a Food Enthusiast, I want to register an account with email and password, so that I can save my profile and preferences. | Food Enthusiast |
| US002 | As a Culinary Content Creator, I want to register my profile with a bio and food interests, so that followers can discover my content. | Culinary Content Creator |
| US003 | As any user, I want to log in and stay authenticated during my session, so that I don’t have to log in repeatedly. | All Users |
| US004 | As a user, I want to edit my profile (bio, avatar, interests), so that I can update my identity and preferences. | All Users |

---

## 2. Dynamic Feed & Discovery

| ID | User Story | Persona |
|----|------------|--------|
| US005 | As a Food Enthusiast, I want to browse a feed of food posts, so that I can discover new dishes and recipes. | Food Enthusiast |
| US006 | As a Food Enthusiast, I want to filter posts by cuisine, ingredients, or tags, so that I only see relevant content. | Food Enthusiast |
| US007 | As a Food Enthusiast, I want infinite scrolling in my feed, so that I can continuously discover content without refreshing. | Food Enthusiast |
| US008 | As a Culinary Content Creator, I want my posts to appear in the feed of users interested in my tags, so that my content reaches the right audience. | Culinary Content Creator |

---

## 3. User Interaction

| ID | User Story | Persona |
|----|------------|--------|
| US009 | As a Food Enthusiast, I want to like posts, so that I can show appreciation and influence content ranking. | Food Enthusiast |
| US010 | As a Food Enthusiast, I want to comment on posts, so that I can engage with the community and share opinions. | Food Enthusiast |
| US011 | As a Food Enthusiast, I want to share posts with my friends, so that they can discover interesting content. | Food Enthusiast |
| US012 | As a Culinary Content Creator, I want to receive notifications when users interact with my posts, so that I can engage with my audience. | Culinary Content Creator |

---

## 4. Content Creation

| ID | User Story | Persona |
|----|------------|--------|
| US013 | As a Culinary Content Creator, I want to create new posts with images and descriptions, so that I can share recipes and culinary experiences. | Culinary Content Creator |
| US014 | As a Culinary Content Creator, I want to tag posts by cuisine, ingredients, or diet type, so that users can find my content easily. | Culinary Content Creator |
| US015 | As a Culinary Content Creator, I want to set posts as public or private, so that I control who can see my content. | Culinary Content Creator |

---

## 5. Analytics & Engagement 

| ID | User Story | Persona |
|----|------------|--------|
| US016 | As a Culinary Content Creator, I want to see basic stats for my posts (likes & comments), so that I know how well my content performs. | Culinary Content Creator |
| US017 | As a Food Enthusiast, I want to view trending food posts, so that I can stay updated on popular content. | Food Enthusiast |
